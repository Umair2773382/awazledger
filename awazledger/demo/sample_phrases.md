# Demo rehearsal phrases

Speak these during rehearsal to test the full loop. Expected parse shown.

## 1. Record a sale
> "Shah Raziq ko bees kilo atta, ek bori, teen hazaar do sau rupay"
→ record | Shah Raziq | atta | 1 bori | Rs 3200 | note: 20kg

## 2. Record another
> "Gul Rehman ko do bori atta"
→ record | Gul Rehman | atta | 2 bori | amount: null

## 3. Correction (the money moment — judges love this)
> "Nahi nahi, Gul Rehman ko teen bori thi, do nahi"
→ correct | quantity: 3 (last entry updated)

## 4. Ask for the accounts
> "Aaj ka hisab batao"
→ summary | today's count, items, total

## 5. Mixed English (shows robustness)
> "Ahmed Khan, 5 kg chini, seven fifty"
→ record | Ahmed Khan | chini | 5 kg | Rs 750

## Backup plan
Hall noise kills mic demos. Pre-record clean versions of phrases 1–4 on your
phone, and play them into the mic if the live attempt fails. The pipeline
accepts any audio — live or file.
